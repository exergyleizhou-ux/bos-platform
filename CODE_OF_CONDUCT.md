# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming research and
engineering environment, the BOS Platform community pledges to
make participation in our project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to creating a positive
environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the science.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.
- Citing prior work fairly, including software contributions
  and methodological lineage.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome
  sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting / derogatory comments, and personal or
  political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical
  or electronic address, without explicit permission.
- Plagiarism — including unattributed use of code, data,
  methods, or text from this repository in academic or
  commercial contexts.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered
  inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scientific integrity

BOS Platform is the scientific software underlying a peer-
reviewed manuscript (Paper 1, *J Clean Prod*, in preparation).
Contributions to the methodology, statistical analyses, or
audit-chain are subject to the same standards of integrity
that govern the manuscript itself:

- Pre-registration discipline: methodological changes that
  affect a pre-registered hypothesis (V1–V13 in the
  manuscript) must be flagged and discussed before merge.
- Reproducibility: all analyses must remain reproducible from
  the frozen seed (42) and the pinned dependency set.
- Audit-chain preservation: changes that affect the paper-SHA
  pin, the OpenAPI snapshot, or the engine `SCHEMA_VERSION`
  strings must follow the re-pin procedure in
  `_reports/PAPER_PINNING.md` §5.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable
behaviour may be reported by contacting the project lead at
exergyleizhou@gmail.com. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and confidentially. The project lead is
obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the
reporter of an incident.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of
Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by other members of the project's
leadership.
